Interactive Audit Services

Comprehensive EHS auditing and consulting services tailored to your industry's regulatory requirements.

Fire Safety Site Audit
Full premises inspection & compliance report
Our certified auditors conduct a 360° inspection of your facility — covering detection systems, suppression equipment, emergency exits, signage, hot-work permits, and documentation. You receive a colour-coded gap analysis and a prioritised corrective action plan within 5 business days.
EHS Management System Review
ISO 45001 / ISO 14001 gap analysis
We benchmark your existing EHS management system against ISO 45001 and ISO 14001 requirements, identifying conformance gaps and providing a structured roadmap to certification readiness or continual improvement milestones.
Electrical Hazard & Arc Flash Assessment
NFPA 70E / IEEE 1584 compliant studies
Identify electrical ignition risks through arc flash hazard analysis, thermal imaging surveys, and equipment condition assessments. Deliverables include arc flash labels, PPE category definitions, and an updated single-line diagram.
Process Safety Management (PSM) Audit
OSHA PSM & EPA RMP compliance
A rigorous process safety audit covering process hazard analysis (PHA), mechanical integrity, management of change (MOC), operating procedures, and contractor safety. Ideal for petrochemical, pharmaceutical, and heavy manufacturing facilities.
Environmental Compliance Audit
Air, water & waste regulatory review
Evaluate your facility's compliance posture across air emissions, stormwater, hazardous waste disposal, and spill prevention (SPCC). We provide regulatory mapping, risk scoring, and agency-ready documentation support.
